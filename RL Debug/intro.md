Introduction

In the previous four parts of “A Chef’s Guide to Fine-Tuning,” we used culinary metaphors to explore Supervised Fine-Tuning, Chain-of-Thought reasoning, and the principles of Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF). We saw how a model, like a skilled chef, learns to move beyond strict recipes and optimize for human preferences.

Even the most carefully trained chef can encounter challenges: a sauce that won’t thicken, a soufflé that collapses. Similarly, fine-tuned models can stall in performance, overfit to reward signals, or fall prey to unintended biases. In this follow-up guide, we translate those kitchen troubleshooting techniques into a systematic framework for diagnosing and resolving RLHF failures.

You will learn how to:

Recognize performance plateaus and stalled learning.

Verify foundational elements—base model quality and preference data integrity.

Determine whether to adjust your current approach or pivot to a different training method.

Diagnose method-specific issues in PPO, DPO, and GRPO.

Address universal RLHF challenges such as reward hacking and data mismatch.

Leverage practical tools, real-world case studies, and quick-reference resources to guide your debugging process.

Whether you’re refining a recipe or refining a model, this guide provides a clear, step-by-step diagnostic “cookbook” to identify problems and implement effective solutions.
